## About me

I'm an architecture graduate pursuing an **M.Tech in Intelligent Building Design**. I'm interested in the meeting point of architecture, computation and emerging digital technologies.

I enjoy exploring how geometry, algorithms and digital workflows can expand the way we design, document and make things.

- 🏛️ Architectural design and intelligent buildings
- 🧩 Computational and parametric design
- 🏗️ BIM and Scan-to-BIM workflows
- 📡 LiDAR, point clouds and photogrammetry
- 🖨️ Digital fabrication and 3D printing
- 🐍 Python and design automation
